# **Manufold**

**Manufold** is a foundation for building **data environments shaped by human practice.**
Each environment — called a *leaf* — is created for a specific community whose members share ways of observing, collecting, and interpreting data.

A leaf translates the structure of a practice into an interactive world:
birders log sightings and lifelists on maps that mirror their field experience;
climbers record routes, conditions, and sends across local crags;
researchers or hobbyists trace their own intricate records of discovery.

Every leaf is distinct — designed around the logic, language, and rhythm of its community — yet all are built upon shared foundations of structure, clarity, and meaning.
Together they form **Manufold**: a growing landscape of interconnected, deeply specific data worlds — each one a reflection of how people understand what they know.
